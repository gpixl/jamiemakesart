I've already mentioned that I don't do commissions for minors, and I will reiterate that here. If you're under 18, please look elsewhere. Sorry!

Things I like to draw:

  • Long, snake-like characters (includes many dragons and lizards, and of course snakes)
  • Pear shaped creatures
  • Fat or generally chubby or round characters (humanoids or body types listed above)
  • Anything involving my sona (It's self indulgent, but I think it's worth mentioning. I love drawing my sona interacting with people's characters.)
  • I like to stylize things in a crunchy way with a small image palette and dithering for gradients. Basically, look at my recent art (or the art on the main commissions page). My art looks like that!

Things I can draw, but I'm less good at:

  • Characters with complicated proportions or shapes (this includes complicated robots or detailed bug-type characters)
  • Human characters
  • Detailed backgrounds

Tangentially: I like to sometimes use blender to help out with drawing snake-like characters or complex 3D shapes. This usually means using blender to render lineart and shading, and hand-drawing extra details on top. If I decide I want to do this, I'll ask you for permission first!

I also like to draw certain types of NSFW art. For more details on this, please type the password into the box.
