Details on things I like to draw

Here are my commission options:

'Flexible' Drawing

For this commission option, I'll expect you to give me some basic direction for what kind of drawing you want, and some kind of reference of your character. (This does NOT have to be a full refsheet! But, the more detailed or specific the reference drawing is, the better I'll be able to draw your character.)

I'll be somewhat experimental with the drawing. I'll do my best to draw your character in my artstyle in a way that I think looks good, and I'll be willing to tweak some small details on request, but I won't take much specific written direction.

It'll be somewhat quicker than the other option!

  • 'Flexible' Full Body character: $60 USD
  • 'Flexible' Headshot, mostly obstructed, or very simple shaped character: $30 USD

'Rigid' Drawing

For this commission option, we'll communicate more directly about what you want and you can give me more specific directions on what you'd like to be drawn.

It helps a LOT if you have some basic visual examples or direction beyond text!! I don't expect you to be an artist to do this. The most simple, crappy microsoft paint drawing can communicate a lot that I might misinterpret in text.

This option is ideal if you want me to make a refsheet for you (and you want it to look a specific way).

  • 'Rigid' Full Body character: $100 USD
  • 'Rigid' Headshot, mostly obstructed, or very simple shaped character: $50 USD

Additional characters, beyond the main one, will cost half the price extra. (Flexible drawing with 2 full-body characters is $60 + 30 (half of 60), so $90).
