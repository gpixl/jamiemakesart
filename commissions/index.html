Commissions Page

This list is pretty important. Please read it once before you commission me. Thanks!

  • I do not do commissions for minors (people under 18). Sorry!
  • You should commission me if you already like the way I draw something. For example, if you like the way I draw chubby characters, or if you like the way I draw snakes.
    If you ask me to draw a detailed human or robot character (or another thing I haven't drawn at all), you will probably be disappointed!
    If the way I already draw doesn't appeal to you, you should commission someone else.
  • I'm not good at doing revisions.
    You can (and should) give me initial ideas and visual references for the drawing, and I will gladly make small tweaks (like color or lineart fixes) - but once I've got the idea down and started drawing, please don't ask me to make big changes.
    Also, please don't ask me to make lots of small changes. (if your character has an exessive amount of small details, I will be less interested in drawing them.)
    (For these reasons, you probably shouldn't commission me if you want me to make a refsheet, unless you want the refsheet to be my interpretation of your character.)
  • If you have a specific vision for how you want me to draw something, please have visual references instead of written direction! This can be an incredibly crappy MsPaint drawing, a google image, or anything you can come up with. Anything visual is better than me drawing dozens of sketches out of trial and error while trying to follow text description. (example)
  • If you are kind and respect everything on this page, I am very happy to do repeat commissions for the same person! It's fun!
  • I draw quickest and best when I'm having fun, so I think it's useful if I just tell you what I like to draw. Linked below is a page with these details.
    If you're wondering about suggestive or NSFW art, also go to that page.

Here are my commission options:

'Flexible' Drawing

  • 'Flexible' Full Body character: $60 USD
  • 'Flexible' Headshot, mostly obstructed, or very simple shaped character: $30 USD

For this commission option, I'll expect you to give me some basic direction for what kind of drawing you want, and some kind of reference of your character. (This does NOT have to be a full refsheet! But, the more detailed or specific the reference drawing is, the better I'll be able to draw your character.)

After you do this, I'll be somewhat experimental with the drawing. I'll do my best to draw your character in my artstyle in a way that I think looks good, and I'll be willing to tweak some small details on request, but I won't take much written direction.

It'll be somewhat quicker than the other option!

'Rigid' Drawing

  • 'Rigid' Full Body character: $100 USD
  • 'Rigid' Headshot, mostly obstructed, or very simple shaped character: $50 USD

For this commission option, we'll communicate more directly about what you want and you can give me more specific directions on what you'd like to be drawn.

It helps a LOT if you have some basic visual examples or direction beyond text!! I don't expect you to be an artist to do this. The most simple, crappy microsoft paint drawing can communicate a lot that I might misinterpret in text.

This option is ideal if you want me to make a refsheet for you (and you want it to look a specific way).

Additional characters, beyond the main one, will cost half the price extra. (Flexible drawing with 2 full-body characters is $60 + 30 (half of 60), so $90).
