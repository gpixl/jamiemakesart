Commissions Page

You should read all of this before you commission me. Thanks!

I work most efficiently when I draw something I like to draw. Generally, you should commission me if you like the way I draw something, and you want to see my interpretation of something.

  • I do not do commissions for minors (people under 18). Sorry!
  • I take payment via paypal or ko-fi. I can link these in DMs.
  • I think you should only commission me if you like the way I draw something. For example, if you like the way I draw chubby characters, or if you like the way I draw snakes.
    I do my best to draw characters accurately, but if you commission me to draw something that's pretty different from anything I've already drawn, you might be disappointed.
    (my art page, for examples on what I draw)
  • I don't like doing revisions and I won't make changes beyond easy tweaks. Sorry!
    You can (and should) give me initial ideas and visual references for the drawing, and I will gladly make small fixes (to coloring or lineart for example).
    But, once I've got the idea down and started drawing, please don't ask me to make big changes, or many, many small changes.
    For these reasons, you probably shouldn't commission me to make a refsheet.
    (Small tweaks to proportions can be made in the sketch phase.) (details)
  • If you have a specific vision for how you want me to draw something, please have visual references instead of written direction!
    This can be an incredibly crappy MsPaint drawing, a google image, or anything you can come up with. Anything visual is better than me drawing dozens of sketches out of trial and error while trying to follow text description. (example)
  • I draw quickest and best when I'm having fun, so I think it's useful if I just tell you what I like to draw. Linked below is a page with these details.
    If you're wondering about suggestive or NSFW art, also go to that page.

    • Sorry if these requests are too strict. I struggle with procrastination pretty often, and it can be caused by some of the issues I covered, so I want to prevent it as best as I can.
    I wanna make sure I'm completing your commission on a reasonable timeframe. I won't be mad if any of my requests make you decide you'd prefer to commission someone else!

Details on things I like to draw How to contact me

Here are my commission options:

'Flexible' Drawing

  • 'Flexible' Full Body character: $60 USD
  • 'Flexible' Headshot, mostly obstructed, or very simple shaped character: $30 USD

For this commission option, I'll expect you to give me some basic direction for what kind of drawing you want, and some kind of reference of your character. (This does NOT have to be a full refsheet! But, the more detailed or specific the reference drawing is, the better I'll be able to draw your character.)

After you do this, I'll be somewhat experimental with the drawing. I'll do my best to draw your character in my artstyle in a way that I think looks good, and I'll be willing to tweak some small details on request, but I won't take much written direction.

It'll be somewhat quicker than the other option!

'Rigid' Drawing

  • 'Rigid' Full Body character: $100 USD
  • 'Rigid' Headshot, mostly obstructed, or very simple shaped character: $50 USD

For this commission option, we'll communicate more directly about what you want and you can give me more specific directions on what you'd like to be drawn.

It helps a LOT if you have some basic visual examples or direction beyond text!! I don't expect you to be an artist to do this. The most simple, crappy microsoft paint drawing can communicate a lot that I might misinterpret in text.

This option is ideal if you want me to make a refsheet for you (and you want it to look a specific way).

Additional characters, beyond the main one, will cost half the price extra. (Flexible drawing with 2 full-body characters is $60 + 30 (half of 60), so $90).
