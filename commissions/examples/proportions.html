Changing the size of a body part in the sketch phase is pretty easy, it's usually just a matter of selecting it and rescaling it.

For anything more than that—like if you want me to alter the shape of something - I will ask you to provide a visual reference instead of just describing it.

This visual reference does NOT have to be anything fancy! You can just screenshot the sketch I sent you and do an incredibly simple scribble over it. It helps a lot!!
