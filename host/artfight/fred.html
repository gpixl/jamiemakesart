Fred is a character from my game Staraway!

  • Age: Adult (~30)
  • Pronouns: They/Them
    (Use any gendered honorifics)
  • Found at: Tealdrop

Fred is a big snake and one of the few residents of a lonely rainy planet. They run a little potion... 'shop'? and they appreciate company.

They're very warm, inviting, fruity, large, and always a great host!

You can draw Fred coiling around someone, but please don't draw anything crazier than that (hypnosis or whatever you might think of)

Also, I don't have a height reference for Fred because I'm not consistent with how large I draw them, but they are big (definitely bigger than a normal person)
