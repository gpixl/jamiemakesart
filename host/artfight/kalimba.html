Kalimba is a minor character from my game Staraway!

  • Age: Adult
  • Pronouns: She/Her
    (Use fem honorifics)
  • Found at: Bistre

Kalimba is a kangaroo and an engineer. She likes to tinker with random parts she finds in Bistre's junkyard.

She always seems to be wearing a grumpy expression, but she's just locked in and usually very focused on her work.

She might seem rude, but she's very helpful if you don't annoy her, and she loves to talk about engineering.
