Chorie is a character from my game Staraway!
  • Age: Adult (~20)
  • Pronouns (etc): She, Her 
    (use fem honorifics)
  • Found at: Zaffre

Chorie is a bird who likes exploring space! She became friends with a certain rabbit after finding them stranded in the asteroid field of Zaffre. 

She usually has a cheery attitude and is pretty expressive of her emotions and values.

Name is pronounced like "Choreography", but without the "Ography"!
