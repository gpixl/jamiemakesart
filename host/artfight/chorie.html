Chorie is a character from my game Staraway!

  • Age: Adult (~20)
  • Pronouns: She/Her
    (Use fem honorifics)
  • Found at: Zaffre

Chorie is a bird who likes exploring space. She became friends with a certain rabbit after finding them stranded in Zaffre, a huge asteroid field.

She usually has a cheery attitude and is pretty expressive of her emotions and values.

Name is pronounced like "Choreography", but without the "ography"
